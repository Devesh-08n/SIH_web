D. Nikolayev et al., IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation, 2021

Reconfigurable Dual-Band Capsule-Conformal Antenna Array for In-Body Bioelectronics

In short

Two mirrored dual-band elements (434 MHz and 2.45 GHz) are wrapped around a tiny capsule. The low band is shrunk using a via short, an impedance step and a meandered line. One switch toggles between array and single-element mode, which moves the pattern null so coverage has no deep gaps.

It was printed on thin flexible liquid crystal polymer (Rogers ULTRALAM 3850HT), designed in CST and checked by measurement.

What we took from it

  • A dual-band array of mirrored elements fits our array requirement.
  • A switch can fill pattern nulls, the same idea as our signal-strength switching.
  • Flexible substrate and a CST workflow we can reuse.
  • Its two bands sit close to our UHF and L-band.
