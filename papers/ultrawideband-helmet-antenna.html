J. Lebaric and A.-T. Tan, Naval Postgraduate School, 2000 · ResearchGate

Ultra-wideband Conformal Helmet Antenna

In short

A vertically polarised antenna covering 300 to 3000 MHz is built into the camouflage cover of a standard Kevlar helmet. It uses conducting cloth, so it is invisible except for one connector at the back. Simulations in HFSS and two prototypes (Mk I, Mk II) were measured.

The operator wearing the helmet did not change the VSWR much.

What we took from it

  • A helmet is a workable, stable antenna platform.
  • It covers our bands, but as one wideband element. Our problem statement asks for an array.
