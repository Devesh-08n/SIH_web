Lightweight, High-Bandwidth Conformal Antenna System for Ballistic Helmets

In short

This paper looks at fitting a light, wide-bandwidth antenna system onto a ballistic helmet without adding bulk.

What we took from it

  • Weight and bandwidth must be balanced on a helmet.
  • It supports our choice of a low-profile conformal design over a whip.
